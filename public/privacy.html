Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a personal portfolio website. I take your privacy seriously and keep data collection to the absolute minimum needed to run this site.

What I collect

Contact form. When you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are submitted to Formspree and forwarded directly to my email inbox. I do not store this data myself. Formspree's own privacy policy applies to how they handle form submissions.

Analytics. This site uses Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand how the site is used and how fast it loads. These tools collect anonymised page view data and performance metrics — no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal identifiers. Vercel's privacy policy governs this data.

What I don't collect

No cookies are set. No tracking pixels. No advertising networks. No third-party data sharing beyond the two services listed above. No accounts, no logins, no user profiles.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font, Google's servers see your IP address. You can find Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel's infrastructure logs standard server access data (IP address, request path, browser type, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. These logs are retained according to Vercel's own data retention policy.
